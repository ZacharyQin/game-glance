# Support page copy

## Suggested headline

Help keep the plugins free

## Main text

I build and maintain free plugins and small tools for handheld gaming and other systems. If one of them has been useful to you, you can support the time that goes into development, testing, compatibility fixes, and maintenance.

Support is completely optional. All plugins, normal releases, documentation, and bug reporting remain free. Contributions do not buy priority support, guaranteed features, or influence over the roadmap — they simply help me keep working on these projects when I have time.

Thank you for using the projects, reporting bugs, testing on different devices, translating, sharing feedback, or supporting development financially. Every kind of help is appreciated.

## Optional recurring tiers

Keep tiers simple, with no perks that create a support obligation. Example amounts are starting points; choose the currency and amounts supported by your account and audience.

### Supporter — about 25 SEK/month

A small recurring contribution toward maintenance and hosting/tooling costs. Includes my thanks and occasional public development updates when I have something meaningful to share.

### Enthusiast — about 50 SEK/month

For people who want to contribute a little more toward keeping the projects maintained. Optional name recognition on a public supporters page, only if you opt in.

### Project sponsor — about 100 SEK/month

For people who want to make a larger contribution toward ongoing development. Same software access as everyone else; no priority support or guaranteed feature work.

## Keep in mind

- One-time contributions are enough; recurring memberships are optional.
- Do not promise a fixed release cadence or private access unless you genuinely want to maintain it.
- Do not make beta access or bug reporting a paid benefit.
- If public recognition is offered, make it opt-in.
- Make it explicit that the plugin remains usable without payment.
