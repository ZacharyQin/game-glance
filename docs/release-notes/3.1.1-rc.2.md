# Game Glance 3.1.1-rc.2 (pre-release, draft)

The 3.1 test build with the status bar and Space Required changes. Not for the Decky store, and the built-in updater
ignores it: install it from the zip on the release page.

- **Space Required.** A Steam game that isn't installed shows the space it needs, labelled "Space Required" in Steam's
  language, right of Played on Home and on the game page. It hands over from Home to the page without a late pop.
- **Sounds.** Opening a game plays Steam's game-page sound, and Play plays Steam's game-start sound.
- **Battery.** The battery is green while charging.
- **Downloads.** The status bar shows a download and its percent while Steam downloads.
- **Your avatar.** Turn on "Profile picture in the status bar" in Quick Access to show your avatar, round, with a ring in
  your online colour, in place of the dot.
- **Quick Access.** The status bar stays on screen while Quick Access is open.
- **Game page.** Steam's top bar no longer flickers behind the status bar as a game page opens.
- **SteamGridDB.** Spotlight Home no longer breaks when SteamGridDB's Square capsules or Uniform featured setting is on
  (#18, thanks @KnifeFed). After you change a game's art with SteamGridDB, Home's accent colour follows the new art
  instead of keeping the old art's colour (#32).

Also in 3.1.1-rc.1: Home recovers from an error and keeps a log file.
